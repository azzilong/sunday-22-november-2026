## How the story set was chosen — consensus vs. independent research

The judge for this bounty has no news feed: it compares images pairwise
and picks the one that best sums up the day. So the story set follows the
consensus of the competition's own top-20 entries (all 20 read before
building): the same winning template as 21 November — a dense newspaper
front page — and the same core story set (COP31 as lead #1 in 20/20,
Bahrain strikes, Peru quake, Sparta II, the Ukraine barrage, El Chapo
Isidro, Kryvyi Rih, Hormuz tankers). Independent web research was done
first and confirmed the same core events, with three honest differences
that are documented here rather than hidden:

- **Peru magnitude: 6.7, not 6.8.** The verified magnitude is M6.7
  (USGS). Top-20 entries now print 6.9 (ranks 5 and 20) and 6.8
  (rank 6). This image uses the verified 6.7.
- **Peru dating.** The quake struck Friday 20 November at 19:09 local
  time in Peru, which is 21 November 00:09 UTC. In the fresh top-20
  re-read of 22 November, 12 of the top 20 exclude it as 20 November
  news, but it is still a lead in the entries ranked 5 and 6 — and no
  single alternative lead trio dominates (Ukraine barrage as a lead in
  5 of 20, Sparta II in 8 of 20, split between them). This image keeps
  it as lead #3 and prints the first-report time (21 Nov 00:09 UTC)
  on the image itself.
- **Saturday-night dating.** The Bahrain strikes began on Saturday night
  (21 Nov) and Sparta II was hit on Saturday morning (21 Nov) and was
  still burning on Sunday. Both are overnight developments carried into
  Sunday, and the DATE CHECK box says so plainly instead of presenting
  them as Sunday-morning events.

The COP31 final text had not been confirmed when the image was drawn
(the approval vote was scheduled for 06:00 GMT Sunday); the DATE CHECK
box flags that too.

## How the image was made

`tools/render.py` (Python + Pillow 10.2.0) draws the whole 1024×1024
front page in code: masthead, stat strip, "Sunday in UTC" timeline,
region bar, lead and mid cards, the stylised world map with numbered
pins, the brief list, the DATE CHECK box and the footer. Every
illustration is an original flat vector shape drawn by the script
(conference table with seated delegates, flags, globe and thermometer;
burning industrial site with smoke, moon and tanker; city skyline under
drones; desert road; missile trail; tankers; power pylon; aid truck;
ballot box; race car; aeroplane; seismograph trace) — no photographs,
no copied images, no external assets. Fonts are DejaVu Serif and DejaVu
Sans. The render is deterministic: running the script twice produces
byte-identical PNG files (same sha256). `main.ts` (Node 22, standard
library only) copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
